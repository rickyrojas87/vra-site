# Editing your website

You can add books, photos and testimonials yourself. You never need to touch
code, and you can't break the site's design — the editor only shows you the
things that are safe to change.

---

## Logging in

1. Go to **https://victoriarojasaudio.com/admin**
2. Click **Sign in with GitHub**
3. A window pops up asking you to authorise. Say yes.

That's it. You stay logged in on that device, so you usually only do this once.

If the popup gets blocked, allow popups for the site and click sign in again.

---

## Adding a book

1. Click **Audiobooks** in the left sidebar
2. Click **New Audiobook** (top right)
3. Fill in what you have:

| Field | What to put |
|---|---|
| **Book title** | Just the title. Leave the subtitle out of this box. |
| **Subtitle** | The rest of the Audible title, like "A Novel". Skip if there isn't one. |
| **Author** | The author's name. |
| **Genre** | One genre, like Thriller. This decides which filter the demo shows under. |
| **Length in hours** | Decimal hours. 5 hours 48 minutes is **5.8**, not 5.48. |
| **Year released** | Just the year. |
| **Status** | Published, or In production if it isn't out yet. |
| **Cover art** | Square image. Bigger is better. |
| **Demo audio** | An MP3 of the sample. |
| **Show sample publicly** | Only matters for In production books — turn it on to let people hear the demo before release. |
| **Audible link** | Paste the whole web address of the book's Audible page. |
| **Audible rating** / **Number of ratings** | Fill both in once it has ratings. Leave both empty until then. |
| **Order on the page** | Lower numbers come first. |

4. Click **Save**

**You can leave most fields empty.** A book with only a title and cover will
show up correctly — it just won't display the bits you left out.

This is worth saying clearly because it caused a problem once: an early version
of the site couldn't cope with blank fields and stopped publishing. That's
fixed. Leaving anything blank is now genuinely safe, whichever fields you skip.
The only thing a book really needs is a **title**.

### About the demo audio

Upload the MP3 and you're done. The moving waveform you see on the site is
drawn automatically the first time the site rebuilds. You don't need to do
anything else, and there's nothing to wait for beyond the usual few minutes.

### To reorder books

Change the **Order on the page** number. Lower numbers appear first.

## Adding a photo

1. Click **Photos** in the sidebar
2. Click **New Photo**
3. Upload the image
4. Write a **Description** — say what's actually happening in the photo.
   "Victoria at the microphone with headphones on" is right.
   "Photo 3" or "IMG_4471" is not. This is what blind visitors hear, and it's
   what Google reads.
5. Set the **Order in the collage**
6. **Save**

**The collage needs at least four photos to appear at all.** With three or
fewer, that whole section of the About page stays hidden — that's deliberate,
because a partial row looks broken. Add a fourth and it appears.

---

## Adding a testimonial

1. Click **Testimonials** in the sidebar
2. Click **New Testimonial**
3. Fill in:
   - **What they said** — their actual words. Two or three sentences reads best.
     Don't add quotation marks; the site styles those for you.
   - **Who said it** — their name
   - **Which book** — optional
   - **Link** — optional, must start with `https://`
4. **Save**

With no testimonials written, that section doesn't appear on the Home page at
all. Add one and it appears.

## Changing the studio and delivery panel

Click **Studio & delivery**. You can edit:

- **Turnaround time** — in your own words, e.g. "Roughly one finished hour per
  working day." Leave it empty and the line simply doesn't appear.
- **Studio equipment** — one line per item, drag to reorder
- **Delivery specifications** — only change these if your actual delivery
  standards change

## How long changes take to appear

**About two to four minutes.**

When you hit Save, the change is recorded immediately, then the site rebuilds
itself and publishes. Refresh the page after a few minutes and it'll be there.

If it's been more than ten minutes, tell Ricky — it means a build failed, and
that's for him to look at, not you.

### One small habit

Every **Save** republishes the whole site, and there's a monthly allowance for
that. It's generous and normal editing won't get near it — but if you're
filling in a book with ten fields, fill them all in and save once at the end,
rather than saving after each one.

Don't let this stop you editing. It's a habit, not a rule.

---

## What you can't change here

Prices, page layouts, colours, fonts, and the wording of the pages themselves
aren't in the editor. That's on purpose — those are the things that would be
easy to break by accident. Ask Ricky for any of those.

---

## If something goes wrong

Nothing you do here is permanent or unrecoverable. Every save is recorded
separately and any change can be undone. If you delete something by mistake or
a change looks wrong, say so — it can be put back exactly as it was.

Don't try to fix it by deleting more things.
