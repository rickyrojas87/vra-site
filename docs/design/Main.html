05How we work together

From manuscript to Audible, in six chapters.

1
Chapter 1
I

Send your book

Share the manuscript, word count and deal type through the quote form. You get a quote and next steps within two business days.

2
Chapter 2
II

Manuscript review

I read the entire manuscript over one to two weeks, prepping plot lines, character arcs and pronunciations. Your pronunciation list and character notes help here.

3
Chapter 3
III

First fifteen

I record the first 15 minutes to set pacing, tone and voices. Once approved, creative direction is entrusted to me. Per-finished-hour projects: a 25% deposit is due.

4
Chapter 4
IV

Punch and roll

Over four to six weeks I record, edit and master the rest of the book in my studio, then upload every finished file for your review.

5
Chapter 5
V

Your review

You note any pickups: technical flubs, mispronunciations or missing words. Two rounds of pickups are included. Additional rounds are subject to extra fees.

6
The end
VI

Payment and release

Once every file is approved, final payment is made before the finished audio goes to the publishing platform for quality assurance and release.
